# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Argue about ideas, never about the
person holding them.

## Our pledge

As contributors and maintainers, we pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise, and learn from them
- Prioritise what is best for the community over what is best for you
- Remember that a question which seems obvious to you was not obvious to
  whoever asked it

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Dismissing or belittling someone for their level of experience
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, code comments — and in public spaces when an individual is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **andreysamuels@outlook.com**. All reports
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and
security of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the
project's leadership.

### Consequences

Maintainers will follow these guidelines in determining the consequences of any
action they deem in violation:

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning, with clarity about why the
   behaviour was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No
   interaction with the people involved for a specified period, including
   unsolicited interaction in community spaces.
3. **Temporary ban** — a temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public
   communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban** — a permanent ban from any sort of public interaction
   within the community.
